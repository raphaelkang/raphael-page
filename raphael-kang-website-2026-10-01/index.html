CPF LIFE. Paid every month, for life.
Your ERA. For the years before 65.
How long and how much depends on the products chosen.

I call it your ERA.ERA stands for Enhanced Retirement Income.We start with savings you already have.We work out together what pays you each month from 60.

What you get depends on the products chosen. Investments can lose value.

Three CPF LIFE plans. Each pays you a different way.

Basic: smaller monthly payouts, more left for your family. Payouts can fall in later years.

Standard: the same amount every month. The most of the three to start.

Escalating: starts lower, then grows 2% every year.

You can also start later, up to 70, for a higher payout. Up to 7% more for each year you wait.

What goes into your ERA

I mix three types. How much of each depends on you.

Annuity. Regular payouts, as set out in the contract.

Market-linked. Regular premiums, invested in the market. Value can go up or down.

Lump sum. One sum, invested for growth and income. Value can go up or down.
